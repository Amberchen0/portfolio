01 — The Character

She blooms by day,
hunts by night.

Nemo is a gentle-looking amphibious spirit who can soar through the sky and dive into the depths of the sea. She lives inside a sunken ship resting at the bottom of the ocean, surrounded by shimmering treasures.

Enchanted by all things that sparkle, Nemo sees herself as the guardian of these long-lost relics. Every day, she busily roams with her companions, searching for new treasures to fill her underwater home.

But don't be fooled by her bright, flower-like appearance or innocent charm — Nemo is, in truth, a carnivore. The delicate blossom atop her head emits a soft, glowing light that lures curious sprites close. And when they let their guard down… she strikes, swallowing her prey whole.
