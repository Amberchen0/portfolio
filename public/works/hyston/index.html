CHAPTER 01 · SYNOPSIS

简介。 Synopsis.

This documentary records how and why Hyston developed slash careers in Melbourne.

There is a concept of game setting for this documentary, thus, it mainly filmed from the first perspective. “I” am a game player login the game called “/” world, as Hyston’s friend to experience her different roles and explore her motivation for slash careers.

We’d love to convey that everyone can choose a lifestyle they like, and there is no need to follow others’ step. You can try some different careers and develop different hobbies, but if you feel tired about this, just stop, logout the “game” and back to your own track. The pace of life that suits you is the best.
